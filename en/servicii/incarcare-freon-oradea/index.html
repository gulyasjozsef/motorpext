Technical details

What does the car AC service include?

Weak cooling does not automatically mean only low refrigerant. It can be a leak, incorrect pressure or a component that no longer works properly. That is why we check the system before recommending a recharge.

  • Air conditioning system checkWe analyze how the system works and whether there are signs of leaks or blockages.
  • Pressure checkWe check pressure to see whether the system can be refilled safely.
  • Freon refillRefrigerant is added only when checks confirm the work is technically justified.
  • Operation checkWe test cooling and system response after refilling.
  • Technical recommendationsIf additional repairs are needed, recommendations are communicated clearly.

Freon refill does not solve every problem

If the system loses refrigerant, a simple refill may not last. Checking the cause reduces the risk of repeated workshop visits.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about freon refill

How long does freon refill take?

Duration depends on the checks needed and system condition, but the work is usually quick when the system is in good condition.

Why is the car AC no longer cooling?

It may be low refrigerant, leaks, compressor issues, sensors, fans or other system components.

Is freon refill alone enough?

Not always. If there are leaks or faulty components, refilling without checking only temporarily solves the visible effect.

How often should car AC be checked?

Periodic checks are recommended, especially before the warm season or when cooling efficiency drops.

Can the car be driven with faulty AC?

Yes, but a system check is recommended. Some issues can affect comfort, demisting and compressor life.
