Technical details

What does wheel alignment include?

Incorrect wheel geometry changes how the car sits on the road: steering can become imprecise, tires may wear prematurely and driving can become tiring. Values are measured, deviations are checked and adjustment is made where the vehicle condition allows it.

  • Measurement on dedicated equipmentWe check wheel position and identify deviations that can cause instability or uneven wear.
  • Adjustment within parametersWe correct angles for more precise steering and a car that is easier to control.
  • Related component checkIf the values cannot be corrected, we look for signs of wear in steering, suspension or tires.
  • Recommendation after measurementMeasured values, completed adjustments and technical recommendations are communicated clearly.
Benefits

Benefits of correct alignment

The goal is a correctly checked vehicle, technically justified work and safer use.

Better driving safety

Correct alignment helps the car remain predictable during lane changes, braking and cornering.

Even tire wear

Correct geometry reduces unnecessary tire stress and helps wear stay more balanced.

Increased stability

The car feels more settled on the road, especially at higher speeds or on longer trips.

Optimized fuel consumption

When the wheels run correctly, the car is not working against its own geometry.

Protection for suspension and steering

Correcting deviations reduces stresses that can affect steering and suspension components over time.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about wheel alignment

When is wheel alignment necessary?

Alignment is recommended when the car pulls to one side, the steering wheel is off-center, tires wear unevenly, or after steering or suspension work.

How long does wheel geometry adjustment take?

Duration depends on the vehicle condition and required adjustments, but the check and adjustment are usually quick for a properly equipped workshop.

Why does the car pull to one side?

Possible causes include incorrect geometry, uneven tire pressure, steering or suspension wear, or braking system issues.

Is alignment recommended after changing tires?

Yes, especially if there is uneven wear, vibration, or if steering or suspension parts were replaced.

Does alignment help with even tire wear?

Yes. Correct geometry reduces uneven wear and can extend tire life.
