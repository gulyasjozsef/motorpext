Technical details

How we check auto electrical systems

Electrical faults can appear intermittently and may have hidden causes. Diagnostics are completed with circuit testing to correctly identify affected components and avoid unnecessary replacements.

  • Technical inspectionThe check starts from vehicle behavior and the components relevant to auto electrical.
  • Cause identificationThe real cause is established before recommending replacements or more expensive work.
  • Technically justified workWork begins after the technical reason, targeted parts and expected result are clarified.
  • Clear recommendationsAt the end, completed work, technical observations and next-period recommendations are communicated.
